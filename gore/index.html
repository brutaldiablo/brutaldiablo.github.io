Brutal DiabloBloodier

It bleeds. So it can die. And it leaves a mess behind.

Every death is different

Every monster has several ways to die. They split, burst, lose their heads. The Cathedral alone has 162 death animations.

162 deaths

Same zombie, same sword, ten different kills.

In Development: deaths from 3D models

Today every death is made from the game's own 2D sprites, frame by frame. Now we're trying to recreate the monsters' original 3D models, so the deaths can be worked out from the models themselves: a new way to fall every time, and much cooler to watch.

Blood that stays

Nothing in the Cathedral gets cleaned up. The blood pools on the stone and slowly dries, splatters the walls and creeps down in drips. Walk through it, and the dungeon remembers where you went: your bloody footprints lead the way.

Fire

Fire kills in its own ways. Monsters burn, explode, turn to ash. Cast a Fire Wall over a bloodbath and the whole room lights up, the Warrior's shadow stretched long across it.

Press H for 1996

Diablo (1996) never stopped running. It's right there under the blood, swing for swing. Drag the divider to peel it back. In the game, H does the same, anytime.

A nod to Brutal Doom

Brutal Doom showed how much fun a bloodier classic can be. So we let Doom's riff loose in Diablo's Cathedral. Sound on.

Work in progress

This is a mod in development, so what you see here is not final. The deaths so far cover the Cathedral's monsters and are made from the 2D sprites; the 3D versions are in development. The rest of the dungeon follows, level by level.
